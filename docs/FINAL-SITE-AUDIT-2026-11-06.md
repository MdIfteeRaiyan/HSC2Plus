# HSC2Plus final-site audit · 6 November 2026

## Automated product checks

- All registered routes, legal/support routes, mobile navigation, 404 handling and internal student journeys are covered by the regression suite.
- Every subject remains available in the correct group; Bangla, English and ICT remain compulsory in both groups.
- Paper switching, chapter switching, note modes, renewable practice, model tests, answer review, progress persistence, print layouts and local CQ-PDF handling remain covered.
- Question-bank safeguards reject duplicate prompts, missing MCQ options, invalid answer positions and unsupported previous-board claims.
- Note safeguards reject thin or mechanically repeated chapter material and preserve code, tables, graphs, images and answer structures where supplied.
- Responsive safeguards cover narrow phones, safe areas, touch targets, reduced motion, focus visibility and horizontal overflow.

## Release corrections in this pass

- Replaced remaining student-facing Business `KPI` shorthand with the clearer term “ফলের সূচক”.
- Practice reports now use the learner's first actual missed-question explanation as a recovery cue instead of a generic revision sentence.
- Corrected release documentation from owner-private preview to public beta.
- Recorded the successful 6 November rollback and prohibited source-only static archives for framework deployments.

## Evidence-gated work that cannot be declared complete automatically

- Qualified subject-specialist approval for every chapter and its complete MCQ/CQ bank.
- Direct approved-edition/page comparison for elective subjects where primary evidence is still pending.
- Authentic previous-board status unless the readable original paper, board, year, set/code, question number and verification record are retained.
- Real-device Android/iPhone/macOS screen-reader and print/PDF sign-off.
- Teacher/student controlled beta, legal review, monitored support workflow and backup-recovery drill.

These are release-governance gates, not hidden interface defects. The product must continue to label them honestly until real evidence is recorded.
